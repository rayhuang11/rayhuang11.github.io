Comparative Advantage of Humans versus AI in the Long Tail

Nikhil Agarwal, Ray Huang, Alex Moehring, Pranav Rajpurkar, Tobias Salz, et al. · AEA Papers and Proceedings, 2024

When can AI replace expert judgment, and when does it stumble on rare cases? We study chest X-ray diagnosis by radiologists and machine-learning models.

Machine-learning algorithms can outperform humans on specific prediction tasks, but supervised approaches require large labeled datasets for each task. We argue that humans retain an advantage on uncommon tasks that require predicting broad outcomes from limited training data. We show that a self-supervised chest X-ray algorithm, which bypasses disease-specific labels, substantially narrows this gap even for rare presentations.
